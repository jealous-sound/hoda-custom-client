Event buffs are no longer capped at <color=orange>+25%</color>.\n\nA few heroes won more than nine fights in ten this way. In PvP only, their stats, status chances and passive bonuses are lowered: Divine Vivienne (-38%), Fen'Harel (-36%), Asha'Bellanar (-18%), Tevinter Banner (-17%), Tempest Sera, Fire Elixir (-14%), Dragon Morrigan (-13%), Queen of the Blackmarsh (-11%), Viscount Varric (-10%), Orlesian Banner (-9%), Dorian (-7%), Red Lyrium Iron Bull (-7%), Briala (-4%), Viddasala (-4%).\n\nA hero now wears only one copy of an item; spare copies are back in your inventory. Opponents follow your trophies, and other players' squads defend with their gear and fortify.
