HODA_NEWS_PVP_PROGRESS_SUBTITLE|Levels, tiers, fortify, runes and gear make a squad stronger again.
HODA_NEWS_PVP_PROGRESS_SUMMARY|A squad you have built up now beats one that was not. Tap to see what changed.
HODA_NEWS_PVP_PROGRESS_MESSAGE|PvP keeps its balance between heroes, but the work you put into them counts again. A hero fights with the share of its strength you have grown: a fully levelled hero of its top tier fights at full strength, a fresh one at a fraction of it, and fortify adds on top.\n\nRarity counts too. A Mythic is stronger than a Legendary, a Legendary than an Epic, and so on down to Common.\n\nRunes work in PvP again, except the flat damage shield. Runes of Power, Health, Speed and Guard count for a small part of their value there, so a rune tips a close fight instead of deciding it. Gear adds up to <color=orange>+50%</color> power and health, and what a single item gives in one battle is limited, so no item wins a fight alone. Event buffs stay capped at <color=orange>+25%</color>.\n\nOpponents now follow your trophies: new squads meet new squads, and the top of the ladder is fully grown.\n\nQuests and the Nexus are unchanged.
